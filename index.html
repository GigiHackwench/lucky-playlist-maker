Lucky Playlist Maker

Describe a scene or a feeling, and get music that fits it, sequenced so every transition flows.

How it works

Start with a picture, not a genre. Say where you are, what the weather is doing, and what is watching you.

"walking home from school in mid-October while ghosts watch from windows and behind garden fences" An example scene description
  1. Describe a scene Write it the way you would tell a friend. Words about place, time, weather and mood all count.
  2. Matched by feeling The scene is read for its emotional shape, and tracks that carry that feeling are pulled in.
  3. Sequenced by key and tempo Tracks are ordered on the Camelot wheel and by BPM, so each transition moves smoothly into the next. Pairs that sit well together are tracked as seams.
